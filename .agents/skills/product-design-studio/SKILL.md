# Product Design Studio

## Standards & Visual Systems
1. **Design Tokens**:
   - Primary: Medallion Gold (`#FBEBC0`, `#F5C542`, `#D4A22E`, `#A97418`, `#6B4610`).
   - Base/Dark: Ember & Obsidian (`#0A0908`, `#100E0C`, `#161311`, `#1F1A16`, `#3A2A16`).
   - Scrims & Glows: Warm amber lighting and gold gradient strokes.
2. **Typography**:
   - Display: Anton (uppercase, leading 0.88 - 1.0).
   - Accents & Navigation: Barlow Condensed 600 (tracking 0.16em - 0.28em).
   - Body: Barlow (line height 1.6 - 1.7).
3. **Interactive Polish**:
   - 3D perspective tilts, magnetic cursor rings, subtle glassmorphism cards, and smooth scroll choreography.
